# Obsidian hybrid workflow (AI + manual)

- **Manual:** judgment, opportunistic capture, curation, refusal.
- **AI:** filing, cross-links, synthesis, lint, proposals.

Lanes:

1. Manual capture → `docs/inbox/manual/` (template `_meta/templates/manual-capture.md`)
2. AI proposal → `docs/inbox/ai/` (template `_meta/templates/ai-proposal.md`)
3. Durable compile → `kb/` or `ref/` after review, index update, validate

Agents read with `_meta/alambic`; they do not need the Obsidian app.
